About

Md Halim Mondol is originally from Meherpur, Bangladesh and currently working as a Research Assistant in the Active Robotic Sensing (ARoS) Laboratory under Prof. Edgar Lobaton. He pursued his B.Sc. in Electronics and Telecommunications Engineering from Rajshahi University of Engineering and Technology (RUET) in 2021, where his thesis focused on power electronics converter development. During that time, Mr Mondol designed and implemented multiconverters, multilevel and transformerless inverters, switching signal schemes, and interleaved boost converters and their control. Later, Mr Mondol completed his M.S. in Electrical Engineering at North Carolina State University (NCSU) in 2024, specializing in power electronics converters, and He is now pursuing his Ph.D. in Electrical Engineering at NCSU (since 2022). His current research interests span large language models, agenting AI, generative AI in cheminformatics, deep learning, and applied AI in engineering problem-solving.
